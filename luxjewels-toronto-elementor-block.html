Toronto Is Canada's Largest Engagement Ring Market

Toronto is Canada's largest engagement ring market. It's also where buyers lose the most money to retail markup. Yorkdale Mall, Yorkville Village, the Eaton Centre, Spadina Avenue's diamond district — these are expensive places to operate a business. And that's before display case insurance, staff commissions, and the advertising spend to get you through the door.

Where Toronto Couples Actually Shop for Engagement Rings

Toronto gives you more options than any other city in Canada. That's both an advantage and a problem, because more options doesn't mean more transparency.

Downtown West

The Spadina Diamond District

Spadina Avenue between Queen and Dundas has been Toronto's diamond hub for decades. Dozens of jewelers operate in a few blocks. The advantage is competition — you can compare stones and prices without driving across the city. The disadvantage is that many of these stores operate on commission-based sales, and the pressure to close a deal can override honest guidance. Some are excellent. Some will show you a lower-grade stone and call it something it isn't. Know what you're looking at before you walk in.

Midtown / Annex

Yorkville and Bloor Street

Birks at Bloor and Bay. Tiffany used to be here. High-end independent boutiques dot Cumberland Street. Yorkville carries premium pricing because Yorkville is a premium address. If you're spending $15,000 or more, you'll find exceptional pieces. Under $10,000, you're paying Yorkville rent on a mid-range diamond.

Across the GTA

Independent Custom Jewelers

Toronto has strong independents doing excellent custom work. Kimberfire operates from a studio near King West with 800+ five-star reviews and direct diamond sourcing. Engage Studio in the downtown core offers a full custom process with resin models before final production. Livia Diamonds near the diamond district specializes in lab grown. Alexis Gallery and Randor have decades of local reputation. These are real craftspeople, and for Toronto buyers who want in-person consultations, they're strong options.

Lab Grown vs Mined Diamonds for Toronto Buyers

Toronto has embraced lab grown diamonds faster than most Canadian cities. Local jewelers report that roughly 7 in 10 couples now choose lab grown, driven primarily by the size and quality advantage at every budget.

Lab grown diamonds cost a fraction of what mined diamonds cost at identical cut, color, clarity, and carat weight. Same crystal structure. Same 10 on the Mohs hardness scale. The FTC confirmed in 2018 that lab grown stones are real diamonds. They're created via CVD or HPHT process in controlled environments.

Canada's Diavik and Ekati mines in the Northwest Territories produce some of the world's most traceable diamonds. If supporting Canadian mining communities matters to you, mined is a strong choice with genuine provenance.

I work with both. I don't push one over the other. For the full comparison, read our lab grown vs natural diamonds page.

Ontario HST on Engagement Rings

Ontario charges 13% HST on jewelry purchases. On a $5,000 ring, that's $650 in tax. On a $10,000 ring, $1,300. There's no way around it — HST applies whether you buy in-store on Spadina or online from a seller anywhere in Canada.

For perspective, Ontario's 13% is mid-range nationally. Alberta buyers pay only 5% GST. Quebec hits 14.975%. For a full provincial comparison, see our engagement rings Canada page.
